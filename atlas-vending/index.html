Snack and beverage vending in Vancouver, Washington

About Us

Atlas Vending is a family-owned and operated vending company based in Vancouver, WA. We own, stock, and service more than ten snack and beverage vending machines at businesses and workplaces throughout the Vancouver area.

We handle everything — machine placement, restocking, maintenance, and customer support — so our host locations get a convenient amenity at no cost to them.

What We Offer

  • Snacks, candy, and healthier options
  • Cold drinks, water, sports drinks, and energy drinks
  • Cash and card / mobile payment at our machines
  • Regular restocking and prompt repairs
  • Free machine placement for qualifying locations

Refunds & Support

If a machine doesn't vend your item or charges you incorrectly, email us with the machine location, the date and time, and the amount charged. We'll make it right, either with a refund to your original payment method or a replacement item.
