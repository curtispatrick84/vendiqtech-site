Payment reach

Accept payments from the apps your customers already use

Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay — no card reader hardware required. Your customer's phone is the payment terminal. Simpler hardware, lower cost, less maintenance, and access to P2P payment networks that neither competitor touches.

Feature access

No upsells. No "Pro tier required."

All in.
every feature, one price

Cantaloupe gates route optimization and advanced inventory behind Seed Pro. Nayax requires higher tiers for full telemetry. With VendiQ, everything is included from day one — no surprises on your invoice.

Know when something's wrong before your customer does

Coin jam detection and temperature monitoring* are built in. Get alerted the moment a jam is detected or a cooler drifts out of safe range — before you lose a sale or spoil inventory. Neither Nayax nor Cantaloupe offer this.
